# Vision

## The shop

**PURO DOCE** — a Curitiba confeitaria making brigadeiro gourmet, bolo no pote,
brownie and cheesecake. Hand-made, fresh daily, "doce de verdade" since 2015.
Real ingredients, no shortcuts (no pó de pudim, no rush).

## The page

A single long-scroll landing page that does two jobs:

1. **Sell the feeling.** Make someone hungry and charmed in the first three
   seconds. Warm, playful, hand-crafted — a candy shop, not a SaaS dashboard.
2. **Take the order.** Let a visitor pick doces and send the order straight to
   the shop's WhatsApp (or hop to iFood / Rappi) without friction.

## Audience

- Curitiba locals craving a treat or ordering for an event.
- Mobile-first in practice — most traffic arrives from Instagram / iFood /
  Rappi links on a phone. The page must feel great on a small screen.

## Brand feeling

| We want | We avoid |
|---------|----------|
| Warm, edible, tactile | Cold, corporate, flat |
| Playful + bold (big wordmarks, bouncy motion) | Generic AI-template look |
| Hand-made, local, honest | Mass-produced, anonymous |
| Appetite-driven imagery | Stock-photo blandness |

Palette is caramel / cacau / cream — the colors of the product itself. Display
type is fat and rounded (Bagel Fat One). Motion is bouncy and food-like:
things drift and wobble.

## The one thing to remember

The giant **PURO DOCE** wordmark hugging a cheesecake, with ingredients
sprinkling down like sugar. If a visitor remembers one frame, it's that.

## Success looks like

- Visitor lands, smiles, scrolls the whole page.
- Adds a doce to the cart and fires off the order on WhatsApp from a phone in
  under a minute.
- The page feels like the shop — not like a website template.
